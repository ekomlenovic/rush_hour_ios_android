# Privacy Policy for Rush Hours

Last updated: 07/10/2026

The Rush Hours application does not collect, store, or share any personal data or user information.

The app works entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Rush Hours does not use any third-party services that collect user data.

## 3. Data Usage

Since no data is collected, no data is used, processed, or shared by the developer.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

This application is not directed to children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## 5. Security

As no data is collected or stored, there is no risk of data exposure from this application.
