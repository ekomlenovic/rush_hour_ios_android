# Privacy Policy for Rush Hours

Last updated: 14/10/2026

This privacy policy applies to the **Rush Hours** mobile application, developed and published by **Imelein**.

## 1. Information Collection

The Rush Hours application does not collect, store, or share any personal data or user information.

The app works entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

## 2. Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules **local notifications** on your device (daily challenge, streak, weekly challenge). They are created and stored by your device only: no push service is used, no token is generated, and nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off at any time in the app options or in your phone settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Rush Hours does not use any third-party services that collect user data.

## 4. Data Usage

Since no data is collected, no data is used, processed, or shared by the developer.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

This application is not directed to children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## 6. Security

As no data is collected or stored, there is no risk of data exposure from this application.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
